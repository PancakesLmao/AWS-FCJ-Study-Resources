#terms 
**High Availability** refers to designing systems that remain operational for long periods of time without interruption.

AWS Global Infrastructure enables high availability by:
- Providing **multiple Availability Zones per Region**
- Allowing services to **automatically recover** from failures
- Supporting load balancing across AZs

By combining:
- Multi-AZ deployments
- Elastic scaling
- Managed services

Applications can achieve **high uptime and fault tolerance** without requiring complex manual recovery processes.
