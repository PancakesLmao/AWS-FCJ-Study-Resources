AWS Global Infrastructure is also designed to meet strict **security and compliance requirements**.
- AWS manages the **physical security** of data centers, including access control, surveillance, and hardware protection
- Customers control:
    - Data location by selecting Regions
    - Encryption of data at rest and in transit
    - Access permissions using IAM

By allowing customers to choose where data is stored and processed, AWS helps organizations comply with **regional regulations and industry standards** such as data residency and privacy laws.
